Refund Policy

When and how fees are refunded.

1. Membership Activation Fees

Membership fees unlock immediate digital access to prepaid tasks, educational courses, and referral tracking. Once digital services have been unlocked, membership fees are generally non-refundable.

2. Unused Wallet Deposits

Deposited funds remaining in your available wallet balance that have not been spent on vouchers or membership activation can be withdrawn back to your verified mobile money number at any time via the Withdrawal page.

3. Contacting Billing Support

If you experience any billing discrepancy or payment failure, please reach out via our Contact page with your transaction reference number.
