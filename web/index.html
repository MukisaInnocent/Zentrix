FAQ

Questions people ask

Is income guaranteed?

No. What you earn depends on the funded tasks available and how many you complete. Zentrix never promises a fixed or typical income.

What does the membership fee buy?

Access to task modules, the learning library and member support. It is a service fee, not an investment, and it is not paid to other members.

Where do task rewards come from?

From budgets that advertisers and clients pay in advance. When a campaign's budget runs out, its tasks stop paying and the app says so.

Can I change my phone number?

Payouts only go to your verified number, so only support can change it after checking your identity. Withdrawals then pause for 48 hours.

Ready to look around?

Zentrix is in its test stage. Accounts work, but no real money moves yet.
