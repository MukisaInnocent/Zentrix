FAQ

Questions people ask

Is income guaranteed?

No. What you earn depends on the funded tasks available and how many you complete. Pexerearn never promises a fixed or typical income.

What does the activation fee buy?

Access to every service above and member support. It is a service fee, not an investment, and it is not paid to other members.

Where do task rewards come from?

From budgets that advertisers and clients pay in advance. When a campaign's budget runs out, its tasks stop paying and the app says so.

I forgot my password. What now?

Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page. We email you a link to choose a new one; it works once and expires after 30 minutes.
