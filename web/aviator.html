Before you play

Please read the Aviator Terms and Conditions. In short:

  • Aviator is played with real money. Only bet what you can afford to lose.
  • Deposit with mobile money straight into Aviator, or move money in from your deposit or main balance. Withdraw from your Aviator balance at any time, with the usual withdrawal limits and charges.
  • Cash out before the round ends to win; if the round ends first, your bet is lost.
  • House limit: if paying every player still in the air would take the house balance below its floor, the round ends at that point and those bets lose.
  • Bots, scripts and more than one account are not allowed.
  • You must be 18 or older. Play for fun and take breaks.

Provably fair · verify a round

Before each round starts, the server publishes the SHA-256 hash of a secret seed. When the round ends the seed is revealed, so anyone can check it matches the hash and produces the same crash point. When the house limit is on and a round ends early because the house cannot pay more, the check shows both the crash point from the seed and where the round ended.
