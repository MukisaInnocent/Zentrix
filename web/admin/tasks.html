Tasks

The tasks on the members' YouTube, TikTok, Ads and Trivia pages. Each task has its own budget, taken from the promotions budget (fund it on Balances). A completed task pays its reward from that budget into the member's task earnings, which cannot be withdrawn yet. When what is left is less than one reward, members no longer see the task. YouTube and TikTok videos play on the member's page. Links to adult content are refused.
